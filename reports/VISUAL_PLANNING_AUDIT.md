## Gaps against the roadmap
The scene plan has meaning/concept/pattern/params/keywords, but no: viewer takeaway and narrative role, element list with stable IDs and required/optional, relationships and hierarchy, asset requirements keyed to elements (image mode), on-screen text kept apart from the narration with its purpose, conceptual motion hints, continuity links, registry-aware icon requirements, validation notes / confidence, and no check that a number or label is grounded in the narration. The page was the generic step page (no stages).

## Decisions (compatibility map)
- **No second planner.** The same Claude call also returns `visual_plan` (schema `LlmVisualPlan`); a project that already has its scene plan gets one Claude call over it (`POST /projects/{id}/visual-planning/run`). The scene plan and the prompts are never changed.
- **New artifact `visual_planning.json`** (not `visual_plan.json`, which the user-editable plan owns). Scene ids use the same convention (`scene_NN`), `plan_scene_id` is the id of `scene_plan.json`, asset requirements carry `prompt_slot` (the existing key of Asset Collection / Image Generation).
- **Backward compatible.** Without a `visual_plan` in the answer, or for a project made before this, the plan is **derived** from the scene plan (`source: derived_from_scene_plan`, stages 1-5 show a warning, `needs_review`); `GET` derives on the fly and writes nothing. Existing tests and fixtures are unchanged and pass.
- **Visual Planning decides WHAT/WHY only.** Motion hints are intents (`show_flow_direction`); `preset`, easing, durations and frames are rejected by the schema or dropped with a note. Motion Planning is untouched (it still reads `scene_plan.json`); `visual_planning.motion_handoff()` / `slot_map()` are the read-only handoffs.
- **Honest confidence.** No probability: `validation.checks` (passed/total of the deterministic checks) and `confidence: supported_by_llm | reduced`.
- Manual only: nothing starts after the Audio Analyzer or Scene Segmentation; `Run Visual Planning` is the only start, the approve button starts the next work, Next only navigates.

## The 8 stages (one module, not 8 agents)
1 Semantic Understanding, 2 Scene Objective & Visual Concept, 3 Element Breakdown, 4 Relationships & Visual Hierarchy, 5 Mode-Aware Planning (one Claude answer for 1-5), 6 Audio Timing & Text, 7 Style & Continuity, 8 Validation & Confidence (code). The card statuses come from `visual_planning.json` `stages`; while running, from the planner's real percentage (0% card 1, 33% cards 2-5, 67% cards 6-8).

## Validation and recovery (what is checked)
Schema (`extra=forbid`), scene coverage and ids, unique element ids, a primary element, relationship endpoints (an invalid one is removed, no element is deleted), registry (an unknown icon becomes a search descriptor, never a trusted id), image mode (every prompt slot and every required picture has an asset requirement; unknown slots are cleared), Motion-Only (no image/video element or requirement: repaired and recorded), exact narration and times copied from the source (the planner cannot set them), on-screen text (grounded in the narration or plan, at most 2, short, safe area; numbers: statistics are errors, spoken numbers count), clutter, hierarchy, palette, repeated concepts, motion words. One bounded repair attempt with the problems, then the derived plan flagged for review; an invalid derived plan fails the step (never a blank plan).

## Real run
`my-video-project-c2e8` (Motion-Only, 10 scenes): real Claude, about 100 s, 10/10 scenes from Claude, one attempt, 204 of 205 checks, 1 warning (a number said in words: fixed afterwards, spoken numbers now count as grounded).

## Source-mapping fix (10 planned scenes for 9 canonical ones), 2026-10-13
**Root cause (confirmed from the files of `my-video-project-c2e8`):** `scene_plan.json`, `plan_input.txt` and `prompts_map.json` were written at 13:03-13:04 from the cut points ("Cut points: 4.58, 8.05 ...", 10 scenes), before Scene Segmentation was used; `scene_segmentation.json` (9 scenes) is from 16:34 and the plan was never made from it. Visual Planning then (1) read its scenes from `scene_plan.json`, (2) took the times from it, and (3) validated the scene count and times against `scene_plan.json` itself, so 10 = 10 and 205/205 passed; the only trace was `timestamp_source: audio_analysis` on the scenes that did not match a segmentation scene, which nothing checked. `plan_checks` allowed any cut point as a boundary and never compared the plan with the segmentation.
**Fix:** Scene Segmentation is the canonical source. `visual_planning.mapping_errors` (one rule, used by the visual plan, `plan_checks` and the pipeline stage): one planned scene for each canonical scene; planned scene k starts where canonical scene k starts (the first at 0) and ends at the start of the next one or the audio end (it covers its scene and the pause after it); anything else is an error naming the scene and the seconds. The visual plan takes narration, start and end from the canonical scene (exact), keeps the pause (`source_mapping.gap_after_seconds`, rule `gaps_preserved`), records `source_mapping` per scene, refuses overlapping intervals, and accepts optional `beats` (visual moments inside ONE scene, checked to stay inside it) instead of splitting a scene. Stage 8 carries these checks (still 8 cards). A mismatch is a blocking error: stage 8 fails, the ring is below 100%, the pipeline stage "Visual Planning" is failed with the message, and approve/Next are not offered. A stale plan (older inputs or rules) is not shown as completed.
**Regeneration:** a scene plan that does not follow the segmentation is made again only on the Run button (`POST /visual-planning/run`): one Claude call from the current segmentation (`plan_input` already says "one scene for each S1, S2 ..."), the planner's checks now refuse a plan that does not follow it, the old files are copied to `plan_backups/<time>/` first, and nothing is replaced when pictures were uploaded or prompts approved (409). Nothing is replaced when the plan follows the segmentation.

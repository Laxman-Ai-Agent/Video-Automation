# Roadmap: from today's output to professional cinematic videos (2026-10-27)

Read after `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/SESSION_STATUS.md`. This file holds the evidence-based assessment of the whole system, the phased plan, and every pending task. Update the "Status" column when a phase moves.

## 1. Assessment (evidence, 2026-10-27)
Evidence: the real render of the test project `my-first-project-01-df7f` (one frame per second in `timeline_thumbs.jpg`, the frames around every cut in `final_qc_frames/`), `visual_planning.json`, `timeline.json`, the 8 generated scenes (`generated_motion/scene_*.tsx`, 2,991 lines), the render profile, the Final QC report, and the stage code.

- **What works:** the autonomous 13-stage run (all stages completed, render verified frame for frame, A/V lengths equal), Telugu transcription + LLM correction, scene segmentation on sentence boundaries, a genuinely good semantic Visual Plan (meaning, takeaway, concept, accuracy limits, beats, elements per scene), generated TSX compiles (0 tsc errors, 449/449 validation checks), provenance/licences, fingerprint reuse.
- **Overall:** the engineering is solid; the videos are ~30-35 % of the goal (a judgment): calm, slow, card-based explainers, not fast cinematic videos.
- **Biggest gaps:**
  1. Pacing: 8 scenes in 59.5 s = one layout per 7.4 s (up to 12.2 s); inside a scene the composition never changes (elements only appear). Pro target: a visual change every 1.5-3 s.
  2. No narration sync: the code generator gets the scene length, not the word times; elements appear on a fixed schedule (`frame - base - i*14`).
  3. Sameness: every scene dark navy gradient, rounded cards, small centred title, content in 40-50 % of the frame; the only "camera" is scale 1 -> 1.04.
  4. The motion library is cut off: `SYSTEM/skills/motion` (16 categories, 35 KB) reaches the code generator as one-line summaries; imports from `components/`, `motion/`, `patterns/` are forbidden, so every scene is reinvented.
  5. Real media ~0: no video clips, 4 small photos in cards (Pexels/Pixabay/Unsplash keys not set; asset ranking never looks at the pictures: a black-and-white historical photo was chosen for "Instagram ad for a clothing store").
  6. No sound design: narration + composition tracks only; no music, SFX or loudness normalisation.
  7. No captions in the video (`@remotion/captions` + Noto Sans Telugu are installed, unused).
  8. Nothing looks at the pixels: the six LLM reviews read JSON reports.
  9. Speed: render 792 s for 59 s of video (13.3x), Whisper 807 s; a 10-minute video = about 2.2 h + 2.2 h on this CPU.
  10. Weight: `SYSTEM/` (agent framework, registries, approvals, skill factory, mock agents) 48k lines vs `VIDEO_ENGINE/` 26k; tests 33k.

### Phase 1 - shots and narration sync (largest visual impact) - DONE 2026-10-27, proven on a real render
What was built, and proven on `run_9272a250` (full detail: `docs/SESSION_STATUS.md`):
- `visual_planning_rules.shots()` cuts every scene into shots of 1.5-3.5 s (`SHOT_MIN`/`SHOT_MAX`/`SHOT_TARGET`) that cover the whole interval the scene holds on the timeline. Every cut falls on the END of a spoken word; the ends of the beats the planner wrote are the preferred cut points; a tail shorter than the minimum joins the shot before it. Derived deterministically: the planner is NOT asked for shots and no extra LLM call is made. Each shot carries `start_frame`/`end_frame` relative to the scene's own frame 0 and the words spoken in it with their frames. A scene whose words allow no cut stays one shot and records the info note `shot_uncuttable` (never a warning nobody can clear).
- Every scene of `visual_planning.json` now has `shots`; `visual_planning.VERSION` is 3 (the plan of an existing project is made again on the next run). New checks (`rules.check_shots`): `shots_present`, `shots_contiguous`, `shots_min_length` (all errors: a failure is a bug in the splitter).
- The code generator receives the shots (`motion_animation.generate_scene_tsx_via_llm(shots=...)`), with system rules 10 and 11: one `<Sequence from=... durationInFrames=...>` per shot, a DIFFERENT composition per shot (not one more element on an unchanged layout), and a word drawn on screen appears at its own `start_frame`. `missing_shot_sequences()` flags a scene whose code has no Sequence for a shot, inside the existing bounded quality-repair pass; the shots are part of `scene_input_fingerprint`, and `CODEGEN_VERSION` is 3 (every scene is written again).
- The preview samples the middle of EVERY shot (`motion_validation_preview.sample_plan(comp, shots)`, `RULE_VERSION` 2; a scene with fewer than two shots keeps entry/mid/pre-exit, so the number of stills stays about the same) and keeps a 16x16 grey thumbnail per still. `picture_change()` is the mean absolute difference of two thumbnails; `motion_validation_rules` checks `preview.shots_sampled` and `preview.shot_changes` (warning below `SHOT_CHANGE_MIN` = 6.0: the scene shows one layout instead of separate shots). `RULE_SET` is 1.2.
- Tests: `tests/test_scene_shots.py` (11 tests: lengths, word-boundary cuts, frames and word times, beat-preferred cuts, short tail, no words, the three checks, the Sequence check, the fingerprint, the sampling, the difference measure).
- **Proven (2026-10-27, run_9272a250):** all 8 scenes compiled with 0 repairs; frames pulled from the real `out.mp4` at the middle of each of scene 4's four shots show four genuinely different compositions, not one layout repeated. `shot_changes` correctly flagged 3 shot pairs with too little visual change (disclosed to Approval/Final QC, not a blocker) - the check works as designed.

### Phase 1 - the original plan
- **Why:** the picture must change every 2-3 s and words must appear when they are spoken.
- **How:** Visual Planning splits every scene into shots of 1.5-3.5 s whose times are snapped to word timestamps (beats already exist in all scenes; `visual_planning.rules.snap_beats` snaps them). The code generator (`motion_animation.generate_scene_tsx_via_llm`) receives each shot's frame range and the word timestamps of the key terms, and builds the scene as one `<Sequence>` per shot with a real layout change per shot. New validation rules: maximum shot length; minimum visual change per 3 s, measured on the preview stills (frame difference).
- **Expected:** a visual change every ~2.5 s; on-screen words land on the spoken word.

### Phase 2 - professional motion kit + art direction (design decided 2026-10-28, optional)
- **Why:** reinventing every scene from scratch gives generic, inconsistent motion.
- **Decision (the user, 2026-10-28):** the motion kit is OPTIONAL, controlled by an option, not a blanket policy reversal.
  - **Option OFF (default):** no kit, no reusable templates. Every scene stays custom code written from scratch, exactly as today. Kit imports stay rejected by the audit (`PROHIBITED_IMPORT_REGEX` unchanged for these projects). Existing projects behave unchanged.
  - **Option ON:** generated scenes may import the kit (camera rig, kinetic typography, transitions, charts, maps, art directions) and the LLM composes scenes from it.
  - **Where the option lives:** chosen at upload, once per project, locked after upload - the same shape as the existing `ai_images` option (`project.json` key `media`, `SYSTEM/core/media_kinds.py`).
  - **Fingerprinting:** the option value goes into the scene fingerprint, so changing it regenerates the scenes.
  - CLAUDE.md's Custom Code Mode line now reads "...permanently removed, unless the project's motion-kit option is on."
- **How:** a small vetted kit in `VIDEO_ENGINE/remotion/src/kit/`: camera rig (pan, push, parallax, rack focus, motion blur), kinetic typography engine (word by word, masks, emphasis, driven by timestamps), transitions chosen by narrative role (whip-pan, match cut, zoom-through, light leak), maps (`public/geo`), charts (Nivo/ECharts), lower thirds, several background style families. Each video picks one of several art directions (palette, typography, grain, texture), so not every video is dark navy.
- **Expected:** consistent professional motion, fewer tokens, fewer compile retries, for projects that opt in; zero change for projects that don't.
- **Built (2026-10-29):** the option is its own `project.json` key `motion_kit` (NOT inside `media`: the Visual Plan fingerprint hashes `media` and does not depend on the kit), set by `POST /projects` form field `motion_kit`, read by `store.motion_kit(pdir)`, shown on the project as `ProjectInfo.motion_kit`, no route changes it. The upload page has a "Motion kit" checkbox (`MotionKitOption.tsx`). In `motion_animation.py` a scene's `../kit` import is rejected by the audit unless the kit is on (`KIT_IMPORT`), the kit is described to the LLM only when on (`KIT_HINT`), and the scene fingerprint gets a `motion_kit` key only when on (so existing projects keep their fingerprints and their code). First kit in `VIDEO_ENGINE/remotion/src/kit/`: `CameraRig` (push/pan), `KineticWords` (words spring in at their own frames), `ART_DIRECTIONS` (5 looks). Tests: `tests/test_motion_kit.py`.
- **Next:** grow the kit (transitions by narrative role, maps, charts, lower thirds, more art directions), have the planner/codegen pick an art direction per video, and prove it on a real render of a NEW kit-on project (not run yet: needs a fresh run).

### Phase 4 - sound design - BUILT 2026-10-29
Licensed music bed ducked under the narration, SFX on transitions/reveals, loudness normalised to -14 LUFS. The biggest jump in perceived "cinematic" quality per unit of work.
- **Built:** `VIDEO_ENGINE/stages/sound_design.py` (ffmpeg only, no LLM) makes `audio_mix.wav` + `sound_design.json`: a synthesised whoosh at every cut of the approved timeline (no licence to track), an OPTIONAL music bed (`assets/music/bed.<ext>` + `bed.json` with source_url, license_name, attribution_text, commercial_use_known true; without complete provenance it is NOT used) ducked by a sidechain compressor and faded, then a two-pass loudnorm to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP. The build refuses (with the numbers) a mix whose length differs from the narration by more than one frame or whose loudness misses the target. `render._render` builds it, `render.stage_audio` plays it, the nothing else in Remotion changed.
- **Freshness (two gaps found by the real run):** the mix is part of the render fingerprint (`llm_review._stage_logic_tag("render")`, legacy `render_inputs`) AND of the render report (`input_fingerprints.sound_design`, checked by `render_video.sound_current` in `current_candidate` and `state`): without the second, the render agent reused the old `out.mp4` because the approved timeline was unchanged.
- **Proven (project `my-first-project-01-df7f`, `run_899f7e7f`, from_stage render):** `out.mp4` audio -21.43 LUFS -> **-14.04 LUFS**, true peak -1.45 dBTP, LRA 2.0, 7 whooshes at the cuts (5.07 / 11.37 / 19.1 / 29.1 / 32.37 / 44.6 / 50.7 s), duration unchanged (59.477 s), Final QC passed_with_warnings, QC review 0.86. Tests (real ffmpeg, synthetic audio): `tests/test_sound_design.py` (loudness, peak, exact length, whoosh energy 20x at the cuts, music ducked >= 6 dB, provenance rule, rebuild only on changed inputs, render stages the mix, an old render is never reused); render_video / final_qc / m9 tests pass.
- **Not done / limits:** nobody listened to it (the whoosh level `WHOOSH_DB` -12 and the music constants are measured, not judged by ear: tune them); NO music was used on the test project (it needs a licensed file; automatic music search is deliberately not built, an unattended pipeline cannot judge a track); only transitions get SFX (no reveal / emphasis sounds); scenes that read `useSceneData().audio` (waveforms) now see the mix. Still in the QC report from before: 5 warnings, "frames at the cut show no content" at 5 of 7 cuts (the empty-cut problem of Phase 0 still shows).

### Phase 5 - QC that sees - 5a BUILT 2026-10-29 (the reviewer looks), 5b pending (findings regenerate the scene)

### Phase 6 - captions and retention - DONE 2026-10-30 (proven on the real project); the starting points below are the record of the design
**Built** (decisions the user approved: option, on by default; spoken-word highlight + small pop; hook in caption style first; a dark plate instead of reserving room in the scenes): `project.json` key `captions` (own key like `motion_kit`; `store.create(captions=True)`, `store.captions(pdir)`: False for a project made before the option; upload checkbox `CaptionsOption`, `data-testid captions-option`; no route changes it). `VIDEO_ENGINE/stages/captions.py` is the ONE implementation of the lines (a caption ends at a sentence end, a 0.45 s pause or 38 / 24 grapheme clusters; first caption = `hook` when it starts before 3 s; `key` = a number or a word the scene shows on screen, from the planned texts of Motion Planning, no LLM call); `motion_animation.custom_composition` writes `captions` and `meta.subtitles` only when the project wants them. Word-timed animated Telugu captions (Noto Sans Telugu), a hook in the first 3 s, emphasis on key terms as they are spoken.

### Phase 7 - performance - render cache DONE (2026-10-30, proven on the real project); the rest PENDING
**Render cache, built and proven** (`VIDEO_ENGINE/stages/render_cache.py` + `render._render`, tests `tests/test_render_cache.py`): the picture is rendered as ONE muted chunk per scene (`remotion render <bundle> --frames=a-b --muted`, one bundle for all chunks), the chunks are joined with ffmpeg stream copy and the audio mix is muxed once (cut where the picture ends). A chunk is the scene's window as `SceneRoot.tsx` lays it out (sum of scene lengths; the previous scene's transition blends into its first frames), keyed by that scene (its JSON, its `generated_motion/scene_N.tsx`, the files its layers name) + the previous scene when a transition leads in + the global part (composition meta without duration/audio, the Remotion `src/` except staged scenes, `public/` names+sizes, `remotion.config.ts`, `package.json`, `assets_manifest.json`, preview/final, flags) + its frame range.

## Audio Analyzer: 14 stages (2026-10-13)
Roadmap: `improvements/Audio_Analyzer_Complete_Roadmap` + the 14-stage task. Chain `transcribe` is now `transcribe -> refine -> cuts` (new step `refine`; `STEPS` order in tests/test_m6.py updated).
- **Stages 1-5** (`transcribe`): probe first (empty / damaged / no audio stream = clear error); Whisper now also stores each word's own probability `p` (additive; older transcripts have none, so nothing is flagged and stage 7 says so).
- **Stages 6-9** (`refine`, `backend/transcript_review.py`): English terms / numbers / project `vocabulary.txt`; words below `p<0.5` (A2V_ASR_LOW_CONFIDENCE) are uncertain; ONE Claude CLI call (existing `promptgen.run_claude`) for the segments with uncertain words. A correction is applied only if its `from` text matches the raw words exactly, it touches an uncertain word, replaces at most 4 words and adds at most 2; everything else is recorded as rejected with the reason. Writes `transcript_review.json` and `transcript_corrected.json` (only when something was applied). `transcript.json` is never edited. Read order everywhere (`load_transcript`, fingerprints, `review.py`, Remotion `build_composition.ts`): `transcript_fixed.json` (user) > `transcript_corrected.json` > `transcript.json`. Claude missing / not signed in / bad answer = raw text stays, stage shows Warning, never Completed.
- **Alignment of corrected words (stage 9)**: the edited segment is force-aligned against the ORIGINAL audio with faster-whisper's own cross-attention aligner (`find_alignment`; model-inferred, not ground truth). It is accepted only if (a) the segment's UNCHANGED words land within 0.4 s (median) of Whisper's own timestamps, (b) times are ordered, inside the audio and do not overlap the unchanged neighbours, and (c) the audio signal has sound inside the word's span; a word whose aligned start lies more than 0.15 s before the first sound is moved to that sound (measured from the signal, reported as warning `start_moved_to_sound`). Otherwise the new words keep an ESTIMATED timing (`timing: "estimated"`, inside the span of the words they replaced), stage 9 shows Warning and `validation.warnings` has `estimated_timestamps`. Estimated is never shown as Completed. Unchanged words keep Whisper's timestamps.
- **Stages 10-13** (`cuts`, `backend/audio_analysis.py`): timestamp integrity (word/segment bounds, order, overlaps), measured silences, loudness (ffmpeg volumedetect; noise NOT measured), unresolved/uncertain-word warnings, final validation -> `audio_analysis.json` (schema `audio-analysis-2`, additive; `GET /projects/{id}/analysis`).
- **Stage 14 Caching & Resume** (shared, not a step): step fingerprints skip finished steps; `refine` fingerprint = raw transcript + vocabulary.txt + audio + ASR model + review version + LOW_P + Claude model; `cuts`/`plan-input`/fill/motion fingerprints include `transcript_corrected.json` and `transcript_review.json`, so a changed transcript, audio or corrected text re-runs exactly what depends on it. While corrected words are ESTIMATED the `refine` step counts as unfinished (its outputs list `transcript_review.incomplete`, a file that never exists) and is retried on the next run until the alignment succeeds; the Claude answer is cached in `asr_llm_cache.json` (same question = no second call).
- **UI**: Audio Analyzer page = 14 boxes (13 sequence + the shared Caching & Resume box), icon + title + percent + status (Completed / Running / Failed / Waiting / Skipped / Warning); a percentage only when real (Whisper's percent for stage 5, 100% when finished). New tests: transcript_review 24, audio_analysis 4, browser_profiles 5. Frontend: vitest 75, tsc, oxlint, vite build clean. Real app: `refine` + 14-stage analysis on `my-video-project-36ea`.
